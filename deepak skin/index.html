About Deepak Skin and Laser Cosmetic Clinic

Expert skin care, advanced treatments, and lasting confidence.

Led by Dr. V. Premraj, M.D., D.V.L., a skin, hair, STD, and cosmetic specialist with 12 years of experience, our clinic provides personalized dermatological care, accurate diagnosis, and advanced laser treatments to help you achieve healthier skin and hair.

  • ✓ Experienced Dermatologist — Specialized care for skin diseases, hair, and cosmetic concerns.
  • ✓ Advanced Laser Treatments — Laser hair removal, acne scar treatment, tattoo removal, and vitiligo care.
  • ✓ Comprehensive Skin Care — PRP, hair fall treatment, HydraFacial, phototherapy, and minor dermatological procedures.
  • ✓ Same-week appointments — because skin concerns shouldn't wait on a calendar.
